## Data left behind

A home that ran an earlier version still holds the collections of the removed Ideas checks (`cards`, `resolutions`,
`checks`, `repos`, `unread`, `meta`, `decisions`) and the Coordinator's `tasks`, and its MCP servers may still have
`synonyms`; nothing reads them. The one-off `scripts/migrations/002-removed-features.mjs` takes them out: a dry run by
default, `--apply` with NetPI closed (it copies `netpi.db` and `settings.json` to `*.pre-002` first and checks that every
other collection kept its documents), `--selftest` against a scratch home. It ran on the owner's home on 2026-10-11 and
was deleted, as migration 001 was; history keeps it at a0d8ffa.
