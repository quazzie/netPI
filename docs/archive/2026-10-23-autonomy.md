# Autonomy plugin: standing grants instead of approvals (2026-10-23, built)

Goal: chats nobody watches — started by a schedule or a webhook (Checkmk/Vakt alerts), or a subagent — keep doing real
work as the owner, and the owner approves almost nothing in the moment. Safety from what each origin may do without
asking, a record of what was changed (pulled, never pushed), and brakes. One plugin, `NetPI.Autonomy`, that can be
disabled: then NetPI behaves exactly as before (the agents run as the owner, the guardrails ask as configured).

## Why one change outside the plugin

Before a tool runs, every `IAgentHook` may block it, make it wait for the user, or rewrite its arguments; one returning
nothing hands it to the next (`ToolBatch.cs`). No hook can allow a call past a later one, and the guardrails run late
(`Order` 10 000). So the guardrails ask a new optional service, `IToolGrants` (`src/NetPI.Contracts/ToolGrants.cs`),
resolved per use: `Find` before they ask (a grant lets the call through, `guard.cleared by: 'grant'`), `Refuse` where
they would ask in a chat nobody watches, and `OriginLabel`/`Grant` for the card's **Always for …**
(`guard.answer scope: 'always'`). Block rules and blocking protected paths are judged first and never reach it.

## What was built

- **Origin** (`Chats.cs`): `user`, `schedule:<id>`, `webhook:<id>`, read from the meta those plugins already write; a
  subagent inherits its parent's (spawning never raises trust).
- **Grants** (`Grants.cs`, `AutonomyStore`): one per origin and rule text; from the card ("Always for your chats" /
  "… webhook Checkmk") or from a refusal in the tab. An ask in a chat nobody watches with no grant is refused and kept
  (`autonomy.unattendedAsks: wait` restores the old waiting for schedules and webhooks; a subagent's is refused either
  way). A grant unused for `autonomy.grantUnusedDays` (60) is dropped; agents cannot create grants.
- **Journal with undo** (`AutonomyHook`, `Order` 10 100, after the guardrails): for the chats of schedules and webhooks
  and their subagents, every call its tool does not mark read-only is a row; a `write`/`edit` keeps a copy of each file
  first. Undo refuses a file that changed again since unless forced. Kept `autonomy.keepDays` (30). The owner's own chats
  and their subagents are never journaled or braked.
- **Brakes**: `autonomy.paused` (tab button), `autonomy.maxChangesPerHour` per origin (120) with one notification per
  origin per hour (`autonomy.braked` → a Windows toast, the only push), and **taint**: a webhook's chat from the start,
  a schedule's after an external tool (`autonomy.externalTools`), may not do what `autonomy.taintedBlocked` lists
  (acting in the browser and on the desktop, changing schedules and webhooks), whatever is granted.
- **Tab** (right panel): Refused / Changes / Grants, Pause, Undo, Allow for …, Remove.
- Tests: `autonomy:` in the Agent suite (8 cases, including "the plugin stopped, it asks as before"), the RPC read-only
  list, `web/test/autonomy-model.test.js`. Docs: SETTINGS.md (Autonomy), PROTOCOL.md (`autonomy.*`, `guard.*`
  additions), PLUGINS.md (`IToolGrants`), UI.md (the tab, the card).

## Decisions taken while building (the owner said "develop it yourself")

- No summaries or digests (owner, 2026-10-23); the cap toast is the one push.
- Unattended asks are refused, not left waiting; a setting keeps the old behaviour.
- The journal and brakes cover schedules and webhooks only, not subagents of the owner's chats: a coding subagent's
  every shell call would have filled the journal and hit the cap. Their asks still go through grants.
- The plugin ships enabled.
